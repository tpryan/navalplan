Typography

Primary Font: Raleway

Raleway is used for headings and high-impact branding. It provides a clean, modern, yet sturdy nautical feel.

HEAVY WEIGHT (900) - BRANDING

Secondary Font: Plus Jakarta Sans

Plus Jakarta Sans is the primary interface font. It offers exceptional legibility and a modern geometric aesthetic for data and body text.

Buttons

Buttons use extreme 50px rounding and Plus Jakarta Sans at a heavy weight for a premium SaaS feel.

Research interface utilizing a "Radar" scan aesthetic. AI-generated spots are highlighted with clean bubble UI.
