Overview

NavalPlan is an invite-only sailing voyage planner that combines interactive maps with AI-driven research. The planning flow follows four natural stages — and the app is designed to move you through them in order.

explore
1 — Discover Browse curated sailing destinations worldwide, filtered by season, to find the right cruising area for your trip.
auto_awesome
2 — Pilot Research Once you have an area in mind, use the Virtual Harbourmaster to surface the best anchorages, marinas, and resource hubs within it.
location_on
3 — Plan Your Stops Pick the specific places you want to visit and build your day-by-day itinerary with dates and route.
article
4 — Research & Go The AI fetches weather, tides, and local facility data for each stop, then assembles a full voyage report to share with your crew.

Getting Started

NavalPlan uses Google sign-in for authentication. Access is invite-only — you must be invited by an administrator before you can log in.

The app before login — the map is explorable, and the Login button is in the top-right corner.

  1. 1
    Log in — Click the Login button in the top-right corner and sign in with your Google account.
  2. 2
    Explore destinations — Click Discover to browse sailing regions worldwide and find the right area for your trip.
  3. 3
    Create a voyage — Navigate the map to your area and click + New Voyage. Set a title, location, and search radius. Add dates when you know your travel window.
  4. 4
    Run Local Pilot Research — Use the Virtual Harbourmaster to scan your search area for the best anchorages, marinas, and hubs. This helps you decide exactly where to stop before building your day-by-day itinerary.
  5. 5
    Add stops & research — Click map spots (or Pilot markers) to add them as stops, then let the AI fetch weather, tides, and facility data for each one.
lightbulb First time? After creating a voyage, a welcome guide will walk you through the main features.

Discovery Mode

Discovery Mode is the starting point for most sailors. It lets you browse hundreds of curated sailing destinations worldwide before committing to a specific route — filter by season to find where the conditions are best for your planned travel window.

Discovery Mode with colored region dots worldwide. Use the month slider to filter by sailing season.

  1. 1
    Click Discover in the sidebar to enter Discovery Mode. The map fills with colored region dots.
  2. 2
    Use the month slider at the bottom of the map to filter destinations by sailing season. The dots update to show only regions that are good for that time of year.
  3. 3
    Click any dot to read a regional briefing — cruising conditions, highlights, entry requirements, and seasonal notes.
  4. 4
    When you find a region you like, close the briefing and create a voyage in that area.

Region dots are color-coded by destination tier:

Voyages

A voyage is the container for your trip. Once you've identified an area — through Discovery Mode or by navigating the map directly — create a voyage to anchor your planning there.

The New Voyage modal — use Map Center to capture your current map position as the voyage location.

  1. 1
    Navigate the map to the area you want to sail, then click + New Voyage. Enter a title and use Use Map Center to set the location to wherever the map is currently centered.
  2. 2
    Set a search radius using the slider (1–150 nm). This controls how wide an area the AI scans when you run Local Pilot Research.
  3. 3
    Add dates (optional) — Set a start and end date if you know your travel window. Dates are required to add stops and run stop research, but can be added at any time.
  4. 4
    Edit or delete — Click the edit pencil to update any voyage detail, or the delete trash icon to remove it permanently.
lightbulb Don't worry about getting the location exact when you create the voyage — you can adjust the search radius slider or drag the circle on the map to fine-tune the area before running research.

Local Pilot Research

Use Local Pilot Research to let the Virtual Harbourmaster scan your voyage area and surface the best anchorages, marinas, moorings, and resource hubs. This is how you find exactly where to stop — before committing to a day-by-day itinerary.

Local Pilot Research mid-run — the radar sweep scans the area while recommendation markers stream onto the map.

  1. 1
    Scroll to the bottom of the sidebar and click Run Local Pilot Research. The search radius circle appears on the map.
  2. 2
    Adjust the search area if needed — drag the center dot to reposition, or drag the edge handle to resize the radius. The area saves automatically.
  3. 3
    The AI scans the area and streams recommendations in real time — markers appear on the map as each one is found. Results are listed in the sidebar.
  4. 4
    Click any marker for details. If it looks like a good stop, click Add as Stop to add it to your itinerary.

Use the filter buttons at the bottom of the map to narrow by facility type:

Anchorages
Marinas & Hubs
Moorings
lightbulb Run Local Pilot Research before locking in your stops — it surfaces local knowledge that isn't on standard charts. Once research is complete, a Re-run Local Pilot Research button appears at the bottom of the sidebar to refresh results.

Stops & Itinerary

Stops are the individual destinations in your voyage. They appear in date order in the sidebar itinerary and as numbered pins on the map.

A voyage with stops in the sidebar itinerary and their corresponding numbered pins on the map.

  1. 1
    Add a stop — With a voyage open, click anywhere on the map. The location is reverse-geocoded automatically. You can also click a Pilot Research marker to add that location as a stop.
  2. 2
    Assign a date — Click a date row in the itinerary. If dates were set on the voyage, the stop's date is assigned automatically based on its position in the list.
  3. 3
    Research a stop — Click the science flask icon next to any stop. The AI gathers weather, tides, and local facilities for that specific location and date.
  4. 4
    View the briefing — Once research completes, a check_circle appears. Click it to open the full briefing with weather, tide charts, and nearby facilities.
  5. 5
    Delete a stop — Click the delete icon next to any stop in the itinerary list.
lightbulb The route line between your stops is drawn automatically in date order. Adjusting dates will reorder the route.

Stop Briefings

A stop briefing is a detailed AI-researched report for one location on a specific date. It includes everything a sailor needs to know before arriving.

A stop briefing — weather, the day's tide chart, and nearby facilities all in one place.

cloud
Weather Forecast conditions, temperature, wind speed & direction, and precipitation for your arrival date.
waves
Tides A tide chart for the day with high/low times and heights — helpful for planning entry into shallower anchorages.
anchor
Facilities Nearby marinas, anchorages, moorings, fuel docks, restaurants, and bars with location data.
brightness_3
Moon & Sun Sunrise/sunset times and moon phase data for night watch planning.

To re-research a stop (for example if your dates changed), open the briefing and click the Redo button. The AI will fetch fresh data.

Stop Research

Instead of researching stops one by one, use Run Stop Research to kick off AI research for every stop and the overall voyage guide simultaneously.

Stop research running — radar sweeps appear on every stop simultaneously while the sidebar shows a progress card.

  1. 1
    Scroll to the bottom of the itinerary sidebar and click Run Stop Research.
  2. 2
    A radar sweep animation appears simultaneously on every stop while research is in progress. A progress card in the sidebar shows the current stage.
  3. 3
    Each stop's radar sweep disappears as its briefing completes. You can click to read briefings as they arrive.
  4. 4
    When all stops and the voyage guide are complete, a Research complete banner appears at the top of the itinerary.
schedule Full research can take a few minutes depending on the number of stops. The app polls in the background — you can keep browsing while it works. Once done, use Re-run Stop Research at the bottom of the sidebar to refresh any time.

Voyage Guide

The Voyage Guide is an AI-compiled narrative overview of your entire trip. It covers the overall route, each destination's character and highlights, passage notes, and planning considerations.

The Voyage Guide — an AI-compiled narrative covering the route, each destination, and passage planning notes.

  1. 1
    Click the Guide button in the itinerary toolbar to open the guide.
  2. 2
    If no guide exists yet, a prompt to trigger research appears. The guide is also generated automatically when you run Stop Research.
  3. 3
    To refresh the guide (after adding new stops or changing dates), click Redo inside the guide modal.
description

Voyage Report

The Voyage Report assembles all your research — the guide, every stop briefing, tide charts, and Pilot Research results — into a single shareable document.

The Voyage Report — map snapshot, stop overview cards, and full day-by-day briefings ready to copy and share.

  1. 1
    Click the Report button in the itinerary toolbar to generate the report.
  2. 2
    The report includes a map snapshot, an overview card grid for all stops, and full day-by-day briefings with tide charts.
  3. 3
    Click Copy to Clipboard to copy the full HTML report. Paste it into an email, document, or notes app to share with your crew.
lightbulb The report works best when all stops have been researched. Run Stop Research first for the most complete document.
share

Sharing

You can share a read-only view of your voyage guide with crew members or family — no login required on their end.

  1. 1
    Open the Voyage Guide and click the share icon inside the modal.
  2. 2
    A unique shareable link is generated. Copy it and send it to anyone — they can view the guide without logging in.
  3. 3
    To revoke access, open the guide again and click the share icon to disable the link.
lock Shared links are read-only. Recipients cannot edit your voyage, add stops, or trigger research.

Using NavalPlan on Mobile

NavalPlan is fully responsive and works on phones and tablets. The layout adapts so you have full access to all features on a smaller screen.

menu
Hamburger Menu Tap the circular menu button (top-left) to open the sidebar. Tap the map to close it.
open_in_full
Full-Screen Panels On mobile, the sidebar and all modals expand to fill the entire screen for easier reading.
touch_app
Map Interaction Tap the map to add stops. Pinch to zoom. Long-press a marker for details.
offline_bolt
Connection Warning A banner appears if the backend becomes unreachable. Research features require an active connection.
lightbulb For the best experience when planning underway, complete your research on desktop before departure. Briefings and the voyage guide are available to read on any device once generated.
