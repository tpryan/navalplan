Overview

NavalPlan is an invite-only sailing voyage planner that combines interactive maps with AI-driven research. The planning flow follows three natural stages — and the app is designed to move you through them in order.

explore
1 — Discover Browse curated sailing destinations worldwide, filtered by season, to find the right cruising area for your trip.
auto_awesome
2 — Pilot Reports Once you have an area in mind, use the Virtual Harbourmaster to surface the best anchorages, marinas, and resource hubs within it.
location_on
3 — Plan Your Stops Pick the specific places you want to visit and build your day-by-day itinerary with dates and route.
article
4 — Research & Go The AI fetches weather, tides, and local facility data for each stop, then assembles a full voyage report to share with your crew.

Getting Started

NavalPlan uses Google sign-in for authentication. Access is invite-only — you must be invited by an administrator before you can log in.

  1. 1
    Log in — Click the Login button in the top-right corner and sign in with your Google account.
  2. 2
    Explore destinations — Click Discover to browse sailing regions worldwide and find the right area for your trip.
  3. 3
    Create a voyage — Navigate the map to your area and click + New Voyage. Set a title, location, and search radius. Add dates when you know your travel window.
  4. 4
    Run Pilot Suggestions — Use the Virtual Harbourmaster to scan your search area for the best anchorages, marinas, and hubs. This helps you decide exactly where to stop before building your day-by-day itinerary.
  5. 5
    Add stops & research — Click map spots (or Pilot markers) to add them as stops, then let the AI fetch weather, tides, and facility data for each one.
lightbulb First time? After creating a voyage, a welcome guide will walk you through the main features.

Discovery Mode

Discovery Mode is the starting point for most sailors. It lets you browse hundreds of curated sailing destinations worldwide before committing to a specific route — filter by season to find where the conditions are best for your planned travel window.

  1. 1
    Click Discover explore in the sidebar to enter Discovery Mode. The map fills with colored region dots.
  2. 2
    Use the month slider at the bottom of the map to filter destinations by the best sailing season for that month. The dots update to show only regions that are good for that time of year.
  3. 3
    Click any dot on the map to read a regional briefing — cruising conditions, highlights, entry requirements, and seasonal notes.
  4. 4
    When you find a region you like, click Discover again to exit Discovery Mode and create a voyage in that area.

Region dots are color-coded by type:

Voyages

A voyage is the container for your trip. Once you've identified an area you want to sail — whether through Discovery Mode or by navigating the map directly — create a voyage to anchor your planning there.

  1. 1
    Navigate the map to the area you want to sail, then click + New Voyage. Enter a title and use Use Map Center to set the location to wherever the map is currently centered.
  2. 2
    Set a search radius — The default is 60 nm. This controls how wide an area the AI scans when you run Pilot Suggestions. You can adjust it after creation by dragging the circle on the map.
  3. 3
    Add dates (optional) — Set a start and end date if you know your travel window. Dates are required before you can add stops and run the stop research agents, but you can add them at any time.
  4. 4
    Edit or delete — Click the edit pencil to update any voyage detail, or the delete trash icon to remove it permanently.
lightbulb Don't worry about getting the location exact when you create the voyage — you can drag the search radius circle to fine-tune the area before running Pilot Suggestions.
auto_awesome

Pilot Suggestions

With your voyage created, use Pilot Suggestions to let the Virtual Harbourmaster scan the area and surface the best anchorages, marinas, moorings, and resource hubs. This is how you find out exactly where to stop — before you commit to a day-by-day itinerary.

  1. 1
    Click the auto_awesome sparkle icon in the itinerary toolbar. The search radius circle appears on the map centered on your voyage.
  2. 2
    Adjust the search area if needed — drag the center dot to reposition it, or drag the edge handle to resize the radius. The area saves automatically.
  3. 3
    Click Research Hubs & Spots. The AI scans the area and streams recommendations in real time — markers appear on the map as each one is found.
  4. 4
    Click any marker for details. If it looks like a good stop, click Add as Stop to add it to your itinerary.

Use the filter buttons at the bottom of the map to narrow by type:

Anchorages
Marinas & Hubs
Moorings

Stops & Itinerary

Stops are the individual destinations in your voyage. They appear in date order in the sidebar itinerary and as numbered pins on the map.

  1. 1
    Add a stop — With a voyage open, click anywhere on the map. The location is reverse-geocoded automatically. You can also click a Pilot Suggestion marker on the map to add that location as a stop.
  2. 2
    Assign a date — Click a stop in the itinerary list. If dates were set on the voyage, the stop's date is assigned automatically based on its position in the list.
  3. 3
    Research a stop — Click the science flask icon next to any stop. The AI gathers weather, tides, and local facilities for that specific location and date.
  4. 4
    View the briefing — Once research completes, a check_circle appears. Click it to open the full briefing with weather, tide charts, and nearby facilities.
  5. 5
    Delete a stop — Click the delete icon next to any stop in the itinerary list.
lightbulb The route line between your stops is drawn automatically. It follows the order of stops by date, so adjusting dates will reorder the route.

Stop Briefings

A stop briefing is a detailed AI-researched report for one location on a specific date. It includes everything a sailor needs to know before arriving.

cloud
Weather Forecast conditions, temperature, wind speed & direction, and precipitation for your arrival date.
waves
Tides A tide chart for the day with high/low times and heights. Helpful for planning entry into shallower anchorages.
anchor
Facilities Nearby marinas, anchorages, moorings, fuel docks, restaurants, and bars with location data.
brightness_3
Moon & Sun Sunrise/sunset times and moon phase data for night watch planning.

To re-research a stop (for example if your dates changed), open the briefing and click the Redo button. The AI will fetch fresh data.

Full Voyage Research

Instead of researching stops one by one, use Full Voyage Research to kick off AI research for every stop and the overall voyage guide simultaneously.

  1. 1
    Click the travel_explore globe icon in the itinerary toolbar to start research for the whole voyage.
  2. 2
    A radar sweep animation appears on the map, cycling through each stop while research is in progress. The research ticker in the sidebar shows live status updates.
  3. 3
    Each stop's icon in the itinerary turns to a check_circle as its briefing completes. You can click to read briefings as they arrive.
  4. 4
    When all stops and the voyage guide are complete, a notification confirms research is done.
schedule Full research can take a few minutes depending on the number of stops. The app polls in the background — you can keep browsing while it works.

Voyage Guide

The Voyage Guide is an AI-compiled narrative overview of your entire trip. It covers the overall route, each destination's character and highlights, passage notes, and planning considerations.

  1. 1
    Click the map map icon in the itinerary toolbar to open the guide.
  2. 2
    If no guide exists yet, a prompt to trigger research appears. The guide is also generated automatically when you run Full Voyage Research.
  3. 3
    To refresh the guide (after adding new stops or changing dates), click Redo inside the guide modal.
description

Voyage Report

The Voyage Report assembles all your research — the guide, every stop briefing, tide charts, and Pilot Suggestions — into a single shareable document.

  1. 1
    Click the description document icon in the itinerary toolbar to generate the report.
  2. 2
    The report includes a map snapshot, an overview card grid for all stops, and full day-by-day briefings with tide charts.
  3. 3
    Click Copy to Clipboard to copy the full HTML report. Paste it into an email, document, or notes app to share with your crew.
lightbulb The report works best when all stops have been researched. Run Full Voyage Research first for a complete document.
share

Sharing

You can share a read-only view of your voyage guide with crew members or family — no login required on their end.

  1. 1
    Open the Voyage Guide and click the share icon inside the modal.
  2. 2
    A unique shareable link is generated. Copy it and send it to anyone — they can view the guide without logging in.
  3. 3
    To revoke access, open the guide again and click the share icon to disable the link.
lock Shared links are read-only. Recipients cannot edit your voyage, add stops, or trigger research.

Using NavalPlan on Mobile

NavalPlan is fully responsive and works on phones and tablets. The layout adapts so you have full access to all features on a smaller screen.

menu
Hamburger Menu Tap the circular menu button (top-left) to open the sidebar. Tap the map to close it.
open_in_full
Full-Screen Panels On mobile, the sidebar and all modals expand to fill the entire screen for easier reading.
touch_app
Map Interaction Tap the map to add stops. Pinch to zoom. Long-press a marker for details.
offline_bolt
Connection Warning A banner appears at the top if the backend becomes unreachable. Research features require an active connection.
lightbulb For the best experience when planning underway, complete your research on desktop before departure. Briefings and the voyage guide are available to read on any device once generated.
